Exercise 1 · Researcher

Tool: M365 Chat (BizChat)  ·  25 minutes

Objective

Use BizChat to benchmark Contoso Medical's procurement thresholds against regional healthcare industry norms, and to summarise the key policy principles from the Policy Manual so you walk into Thursday's Finance Committee prepared.

Task 1 — Industry Benchmarking

Open M365 Chat and use the prompt below to research healthcare procurement benchmarks.

I work in Finance at a private hospital group in Malaysia. Summarise best-practice procurement thresholds for private healthcare groups in Southeast Asia — covering: single-quote limits, RFQ/tender thresholds, and standard auto-renewal notice periods for vendor contracts. Present your answer as a comparison table and highlight where practices vary by hospital group size.

What to look for

  • How does our RM 10K single-quote limit compare to peers?
  • Is our 90-day auto-renewal notice standard or above average?
  • Are our liability cap standards (12 months minimum) typical?

Task 2 — Policy Manual Summary

Attach the Policy Manual file (05_ContosoMedical_FinanceProcurementPolicies.docx) to BizChat and use the prompt below.

Using the attached Finance & Procurement Policy Manual, provide a structured one-page executive summary covering: (1) CAPEX approval authority thresholds, (2) budget variance escalation rules, (3) procurement tender thresholds, and (4) the three most important contract standards (liability cap, auto-renewal notice, price escalation). Format as a table for each section.

Discussion

Share your summary with the group. Does everyone have the same three contract standards? What surprised you?

Task 3 — Gap Analysis Setup

Based on the Policy Manual I attached, draft five questions I should ask when reviewing any new vendor contract to ensure it complies with Contoso Medical's policies. Make the questions specific enough that a non-lawyer Finance Manager could use them as a checklist.

Save this checklist — you will use it in Exercise 3.

Exercise 2 · Financial Analysis

Tool: Copilot in Excel  ·  30 minutes

Objective

Open the Budget Variance workbook in Excel. Use Copilot in Excel to surface the worst-performing departments, understand the root causes, score the supplier quotations, and generate ready-to-use CFO commentary for Thursday's committee pack.

Task 1 — Budget Variance Analysis

Open 01_ContosoMedical_BudgetVariance.xlsx in Excel. Navigate to the Monthly_PnL sheet and open Copilot (Home ribbon → Copilot).

Analyse this budget variance data. Identify the top 3 departments with the largest unfavourable variances year-to-date. For each, calculate the variance as a percentage of budget and flag which departments have exceeded the 10% escalation threshold per our internal policy. Present the results as a table with a RAG status column.

Expected Finding

Look for departments where the adverse variance exceeds 10% — our policy requires a CFO review and corrective action plan for these.

Task 2 — Supplier Quotation Scoring

Switch to 02_ContosoMedical_SupplierQuotations.xlsx, Scoring_Guide sheet.

Review the supplier scoring matrix on this sheet. Identify which supplier(s) should be automatically disqualified based on the disqualification criteria shown. Then re-rank the remaining qualifying suppliers by overall weighted score. Explain your reasoning for any disqualification.

Key Question

Which supplier is disqualified and why? Does the disqualification change your recommendation?

Task 3 — CFO Commentary Draft

Based on the budget variance data in the Monthly_PnL sheet, write a 150-word CFO commentary for the Q1 Finance Committee pack. The commentary should: (1) state the Group's overall budget position, (2) highlight the two most significant unfavourable variances by department, (3) note one area of positive performance, and (4) state the next management action. Use formal finance language.
⭐ Bonus — Analyst Agent
If you finish early, try asking Copilot: "Create a pivot table showing revenue vs budget by department for all 12 months, with conditional formatting to highlight months where variance exceeds 10%." Then use Copilot to generate a chart from the pivot table.

Open 03_ContosoMedical_VendorContract.docx in Word. Open Copilot from the Home ribbon.

Review this vendor contract and identify all clauses that could be considered HIGH or MEDIUM risk for Contoso Medical. For each risk clause, state: (1) the clause number, (2) the risk description, (3) why it is problematic, and (4) what the recommended standard should be. Present as a table.

Using the risk clauses you identified, draft a one-page Contract Risk Summary for the Finance Committee. Prompting Tips

The GCSE framework + Finance-specific prompt library

The GCSE Prompting Framework

G — Goal

What do you want Copilot to produce? Be specific about the output type: table, email, summary, analysis.

C — Context

Who are you? What is the situation? The more context you give, the more relevant the output.

S — Source

Reference the specific file, data, or knowledge Copilot should use. Never assume it will find it automatically.

E — Expectation

What format, tone, length, or structure do you expect? E.g. "as a table", "under 150 words", "board-report style".

Example (Bad vs Good):
❌ "Summarise the contract."
✅ "Using the attached TechMed contract (Source), identify all clauses that breach Contoso Medical's standard contract policy (Context), and present them as a risk table with clause number, risk level, and recommended action (Expectation). I am the Finance Manager preparing a report for the CFO (Goal + Context)."

Finance Prompt Library

Budget Variance Commentary

"Write a CFO commentary for [department] showing an adverse variance of [X]% vs budget in [month]. Explain likely causes and management actions. Keep it under 100 words."

Contract Risk Scan

"Review this contract and flag any clauses relating to: liability cap, auto-renewal, price escalation, data ownership, and IP. Rate each as HIGH/MEDIUM/LOW risk."

Supplier Comparison

"Compare these [N] supplier quotes based on: price, delivery lead time, quality certification, and payment terms. Recommend the best value option with justification."

Board Summary Draft

"Draft a one-page board summary covering [topic]. Include: executive summary (3 sentences), key findings (bullet list), financial impact, and recommended board resolution."

Policy Q&A

"Based on the [Policy Manual], what is the correct approval process for [scenario]? Quote the relevant policy section and state exactly who must approve."

Action Item Extraction

"Extract all action items from this meeting transcript. For each, provide: action description, owner, due date, and priority (High/Medium/Low). Present as a table."
