Welcome

This immersion experience puts you in the role of Nurul, Group Finance Manager at Contoso Medical Group, a fast-growing private healthcare network across Malaysia. Over 2 hours you will work through four exercises that mirror real finance and procurement challenges: researching industry benchmarks, analysing budget variances, reviewing vendor contracts, building a Finance Committee deck, and finally creating your own AI agent grounded in your organisation's policies.

Exercise 1 · Researcher

Tools: Researcher · Model Council · Copilot in Word  ·  15 minutes (Show & Tell)

📝 Note: Exercise 1 is a Show & Tell. The Researcher Agent (including Critique and Model Council) requires tenant-level enablement and may not be available in all environments. If you can see Researcher in your Copilot Chat agents panel, follow along and try the prompts on your own device. If it is not visible, sit back and follow the facilitator's live walkthrough — this exercise is a showcase of what becomes available once Researcher is enabled at the tenant level.

Objective

Use Researcher's Critique mode to benchmark Contoso Medical's procurement governance against regional peers, then switch to Model Council for a multi-model view on healthcare finance risks, and finally use Copilot in Word to turn the research into a CFO briefing note.

Step 1 · Open the Researcher Agent

1
Go to Copilot Chat and click Researcher in the agents panel on the right.
2
You will use two modes across Tasks 1 and 2:
🔍 Critique (Auto)

The default mode. One model researches and drafts; a second independently reviews source reliability and evidence grounding before the final report is delivered. Every key claim is anchored to a citable source.

⚖️ Model Council

Multiple models each run your prompt independently and produce full standalone reports. A judge model then writes a cover letter highlighting where models agree, diverge, and what unique insight each brings.

Task 1 · Critique: Industry Benchmarking

Keep the model selector on Auto (this is Critique). Use the prompt below:

I am the Head of Finance at Contoso Medical Group, a private hospital group in Malaysia with four facilities and approximately RM 280 million in annual operating expenditure. Our CFO has asked me to benchmark our procurement governance framework before Thursday's Finance Committee review. I need you to research how comparable private hospital groups in Southeast Asia, specifically IHH Healthcare, KPJ Healthcare, Ramsay Sime Darby, and Bangkok Dusit Medical Services, structure their procurement thresholds, including single-quote purchase limits, the value at which a formal RFQ requiring at least three supplier quotes is triggered, open tender thresholds, standard notice periods required to prevent auto-renewal of vendor contracts, and typical liability cap durations in technology and managed-services agreements. Present your findings as a comparison table with each hospital group as a column and each policy dimension as a row. In a separate paragraph below the table, highlight the two or three areas where our practice is likely to differ most significantly from regional peers, and flag any areas where tighter controls are generally considered better governance practice in the healthcare sector.

Check the citations in the report. Critique enforces strict evidence grounding, requiring every key claim to be anchored to a reliable, citable source before the final output is produced.

Task 2 · Model Council: Financial Governance Risks

Click the model picker in Researcher and switch to Model Council. Each model will run your prompt independently and produce its own full report. Use the prompt below:

I am the Head of Finance at Contoso Medical Group, a private hospital group in Malaysia. I need a strategic risk assessment of the top financial governance and procurement risks facing private hospital groups in Malaysia over the next two years. Consider the following dimensions: rising medical consumable costs and supply chain concentration risk following post-pandemic sourcing shifts, regulatory pressure from the Ministry of Health on private hospital billing transparency and procurement compliance, vendor contract risk particularly around technology and managed-services agreements where liability and data ownership terms are often unfavourable, budget variance management as clinical department heads push for greater procurement autonomy, and ESG-related procurement obligations emerging from Bursa listing requirements for healthcare groups. For each risk, assess the likelihood and potential financial impact, describe how leading hospital groups in the region are mitigating it, and suggest one concrete governance action Contoso Medical should prioritise in the next six months. Present as a structured risk register with a one-paragraph executive summary at the top.

Read the cover letter, written by a dedicated judge model that compares both reports and highlights where they agree, where they diverge, and the unique insight each model brings. That is where the real value of Model Council shows.

Task 3 · Word: CFO Briefing Note

First, get your Task 1 Critique research into Word:

Critique output: In your Researcher report, click the Export button → Convert to Word. The document saves automatically to your OneDrive. Open it from OneDrive.
Model Council output: Choose the report you prefer. Select all → Copy. Open a blank Word document, paste, and save to your OneDrive.

Open the Word document. In the Home ribbon → Copilot pane, use the prompt below to draft the CFO briefing note:

Based on the research content in this document, draft a one-page CFO briefing note for the Contoso Medical Group Finance Committee. Structure it as follows: open with a one-paragraph executive summary stating the overall governance maturity of our procurement framework relative to regional peers and the headline financial governance risk we face in the next 12 months. Follow with a benchmarking summary table showing where Contoso Medical stands on the key procurement threshold dimensions compared to regional peer groups, using a simple Above Peer, In Line, or Below Peer rating for each dimension. Then write a section covering the top three governance risks that require action this financial year, each with a plain-English explanation of the risk and a specific recommended action with a proposed owner and timeline. Close with a next steps paragraph recommending the two or three policy or process changes the CFO should prioritise before the next Board Audit Committee meeting. Write all narrative in formal business English suitable for a Board-level audience. Use square bracket placeholders for any data point not confirmed in the research. Target length is 350 to 400 words.

Exercise 2 · Financial Analysis

Tools: Copilot in Excel · Analyst Agent  ·  30 minutes

Objective

Open the Budget Variance workbook in Excel. Use Copilot in Excel to surface overspending departments, draft CFO commentary, and build an executive finance dashboard, then hand the same data to the Analyst Agent for deeper AI-driven analysis.

Task 1 · Variance Analysis & CFO Commentary

Open 01_ContosoMedical_BudgetVariance.xlsx in Excel. Navigate to the Monthly_PnL sheet and open Copilot from the Home ribbon → Copilot.

I am the Group Finance Manager at Contoso Medical Group preparing the Q1 Finance Committee board pack, which the CFO needs by end of day Thursday. Using the Monthly_PnL sheet in this workbook, I need two outputs placed together in a new sheet called Q1 Board Summary. First, produce a Finance Committee-ready variance summary table covering every department, showing actual expenditure year-to-date, budget year-to-date, the RM variance, and the variance as a percentage, with a RAG status column where Red means over 10% adverse, Amber means 5 to 10% adverse, and Green means within 5% of budget. Flag any department where the variance has breached the 10% threshold that triggers a mandatory corrective action plan under our internal policy, and for those departments add a one-sentence explanation of the likely cost driver based on the data visible in the sheet. Second, immediately below the table in the same Q1 Board Summary sheet, draft the CFO narrative commentary for the board pack. This should open by stating the Group's overall Q1 cost position in both RM and percentage terms, discuss the two departments with the most significant adverse variances by name and explain what is driving each overspend, briefly acknowledge the best-performing department to give a balanced view, explicitly state which departments have triggered the corrective action threshold, and close with a clear statement of the management actions proposed and the timeline for resolution. Write the commentary in formal continuous prose, no bullet points, 200 to 220 words, suitable for a Board Audit Committee pack.

Task 2 · Finance Committee Dashboard

Stay in 01_ContosoMedical_BudgetVariance.xlsx with Copilot in Excel open. Ask Copilot to build an executive dashboard sheet with no pivot table or chart setup needed.

💡 Note: Copilot in Excel will create the new sheet, build the charts, and add the summary table. Print layout formatting (A4 landscape) may need a quick manual tidy after — use Page Layout → Fit to Page if needed.

I am preparing a Finance Committee board pack for Contoso Medical Group and I need a visual executive dashboard built directly in this workbook so it can be printed and included as a single-page insert for the committee. Using the data in the Monthly_PnL sheet, create a new sheet called Dashboard and build the following elements on it. At the top of the page create a KPI summary row with three clearly labelled headline boxes showing total Group actual expenditure year-to-date in RM, total Group budget year-to-date in RM, and the overall variance expressed as both a RM figure and a percentage with a RAG indicator so the committee sees the Group position in seconds. Below the KPI row create a horizontal bar chart ranking every department by their year-to-date adverse variance from worst to best, with RAG colour coding applied directly to the bars: Red for over 10% adverse, Amber for 5 to 10%, Green for within budget, so the committee can immediately see which departments need attention without reading a table. Add a second chart showing the monthly trend of total Group actual expenditure versus total budget for all 12 months as a dual-line chart, so the committee can see whether the gap is widening or narrowing over time and assess whether the variance is structural or seasonal. Finally add a compact summary table below the charts listing only the Red-flagged departments with columns for department name, YTD variance in RM, variance percentage, and a status cell reading Corrective Action Required. Format the entire dashboard to fit and print cleanly on a single A4 landscape page so it can be inserted directly into the board pack without resizing.

Task 3 · Analyst Agent

The Analyst Agent is a built-in agent in Copilot Chat that writes and runs its own code to analyse data, with no Excel skills or formula setup needed. Open Copilot Chat, select Analyst from the agents panel, and upload 01_ContosoMedical_BudgetVariance.xlsx.

I am the Group Finance Manager at Contoso Medical Group and I am uploading the Q1 budget variance workbook to prepare for Thursday's Finance Committee. I need the Analyst Agent to run a full quantitative analysis on the Monthly_PnL sheet and produce six specific outputs. First, build a complete pivot table showing actual expenditure versus budget for every department across all 12 months with a variance column, a variance percentage column, and RAG conditional formatting applied automatically so Red cells indicate over 10% adverse variance, Amber indicates 5 to 10%, and Green indicates within budget. Second, generate a horizontal bar chart ranking every department by total year-to-date adverse variance from worst to best, with RAG colour coding on the bars, so the committee can see the full picture at a glance. Third, produce a dual-line chart showing total Group actual expenditure versus total budget for each month across the full year, so the committee can see whether the variance gap is widening, narrowing, or seasonal. Fourth, produce a stacked bar chart breaking down monthly actual expenditure by department for the year so leadership can see which departments are driving cost growth over time. Fifth, analyse the monthly trend data for every department and identify those that have overspent against budget for three or more consecutive months, because a persistent multi-month pattern indicates a structural budget problem rather than a one-off timing issue, and present these in a separate flagged table with the department name, number of consecutive months of overspend, and total cumulative overspend amount. Sixth, calculate the projected year-end financial exposure if every department currently in breach of the 10% threshold continues to overspend at the same average monthly run rate for the remaining months of the financial year, and present this as a department-by-department exposure table with a grand total worst-case RM figure at the bottom for the CFO briefing.

Exercise 3 · Contract & Reporting

Tools: Copilot in Word · Copilot in PowerPoint · Cowork  ·  30 minutes

Objective

Review the TechMed vendor contract for policy breaches using Copilot in Word, then turn your findings into a Finance Committee presentation using Copilot in PowerPoint, and finally use Cowork to delegate five wrap-up tasks to Copilot simultaneously, including two document outputs.

Task 1 · Contract Risk Review

Open 02_ContosoMedical_VendorContract.docx in Word. Open Copilot from the Home ribbon.

I am the Group Finance Manager at Contoso Medical Group and I need to review this TechMed Healthcare Solutions contract before raising it at Thursday's Finance Committee, because the Audit Committee has flagged it as potentially containing unfavourable terms that may expose us to financial and operational risk. Read the full contract and identify every clause that creates a financial, legal, or operational risk for Contoso Medical. For each risk clause, quote the exact problematic contract language so the finding is evidence-based, explain in plain English what the risk is and why it matters specifically to a private hospital group operating in Malaysia, estimate the financial or operational exposure quantified in RM wherever the contract value and clause terms make this calculable, describe what the market-standard position for this type of clause should be in a managed-services agreement of this size, and suggest revised alternative wording that would adequately protect Contoso Medical's interests. Rate each risk as HIGH, MEDIUM, or LOW based on the magnitude of potential financial exposure and the likelihood of the risk being triggered during the contract term. Present the complete findings as a risk register table sorted by risk level with HIGH risks first, so the Finance Committee sees the most critical issues immediately and can make an informed decision on whether to approve, conditionally approve, or send the contract back for renegotiation.

Task 2 · Finance Committee Deck

Open a blank presentation in PowerPoint. Open Copilot from the Home ribbon and use the prompt below to generate the Finance Committee briefing deck from your Task 1 findings.

I am the Group Finance Manager at Contoso Medical Group and I need to build a Finance Committee briefing deck for Thursday's committee meeting that covers both our Q1 financial position and the TechMed contract risk findings. Create a presentation with six slides using a professional navy and white corporate design throughout. Slide one should be a clean title slide with the title Q1 Finance and Procurement Committee Review, the organisation name Contoso Medical Group, the audience Finance Committee, and the meeting date. Slide two should present a Group Financial Snapshot with three headline KPI boxes showing our overall Q1 budget position in RM, the largest single department adverse variance with the department name, and the number of departments that have breached the mandatory 10% corrective action threshold, each with a RAG status indicator so the committee absorbs the key numbers in under ten seconds. Slide three should cover the top three overspending departments with one column per department showing the variance amount in RM, the variance percentage, and a one-line root cause so the committee can see where attention is needed. Slide four should present the TechMed contract risk register as a compact table with clause number, risk rating, issue description, and RM exposure for each of the four identified risk clauses, with the combined worst-case HIGH-risk exposure shown as a total row at the bottom. Slide five should list three specific recommended actions with a named owner and a deadline for each. Slide six should be a Decision Required slide that states plainly the two decisions the Finance Committee is being asked to make at this meeting: one on the budget corrective action plans and one on the TechMed contract. Keep all slide text at headline level with no full sentences in bullet points, and flag any figure with a square bracket placeholder where data needs to be confirmed.

Exercise 4 · Agent Builder

Tool: Microsoft 365 Agent Builder  ·  20 minutes

Objective

Use the built-in Agent Builder in Microsoft 365 Copilot to build a Finance Policy Advisor agent in under 20 minutes, grounded in the Contoso Medical Finance & Procurement Policy Manual, so anyone on the team can get instant, policy-accurate answers without searching through 40 pages of documentation.

How Agent Builder works

💬
1. Describe

Type what you want the agent to do. Copilot writes the name, description & instructions for you

⚙️
2. Configure

Add your knowledge files, turn on Word/Excel/PowerPoint creation, images & code interpreter

🧪
3. Test

Chat with your agent in the preview pane and refine the instructions until it behaves correctly

🚀
4. Create & Share

Click Create to publish. Your agent is live and ready to share with your team in Copilot Chat

📚 Build: Finance Policy Advisor

Answers questions about approval thresholds, escalation rules, and procurement policy. Grounded in your internal policy document, it only answers from what the policy states.

1
Open Agent Builder. Go to M365 Copilot Chat, then click Create an agent in the right-hand panel. You will land on the Describe tab.
2
Describe your agent. Paste the prompt below into the Describe box. Agent Builder will automatically generate the agent name, description, and full instructions with no manual setup needed.
I want to build a Finance Policy Advisor agent for Contoso Medical Group that helps the Finance and Procurement teams get fast, accurate answers to policy questions without having to search through the full policy manual. The agent should always cite the specific policy section number when answering, never guess or answer beyond what the policy document states, flag any request that is likely to require CFO or Board approval based on the approval thresholds in the policy, and respond in plain English suitable for finance staff who are not legal experts. When a question falls outside the scope of the policy document, the agent should say so clearly and suggest who to contact for guidance rather than attempting an answer.
✅ Review the generated name, description and instructions. Adjust wording if needed. Then click the Configure tab at the top.
3
Configure tab. Click Configure in the tab bar and set the following:
  • Knowledge: Click Add knowledge → upload 04_ContosoMedical_FinanceProcurementPolicies.docx (or paste the SharePoint/OneDrive link)
  • ✅ Toggle on Create documents, charts, and code
  • ✅ Toggle on Create images
4
Test in the preview pane. Use the right-hand chat preview to test these prompts:
What is the approval authority for a capital expenditure item of RM 350,000. Can a department head approve this or does it require CFO sign-off?
A department head wants to purchase RM 80,000 of medical equipment from a single supplier without going to open tender. Is this allowed under our procurement policy, and if not, what process should be followed?
We are reviewing a vendor contract that has a 60-day auto-renewal notice period and a liability cap of six months' fees. Are either of these terms compliant with our standard contract policy, and what is the required standard for each?
💡 Check that the agent cites a specific policy section in each answer. If it doesn't, go back to Configure and refine the instructions.
5
Create & Share. Click Create to publish your agent. Copy the agent link and share it with your Finance and Procurement teams. They can access it directly in M365 Copilot Chat, no setup required. Note: recipients must have an active M365 Copilot licence to use the agent.

I need you to draft the management commentary that will accompany the accounts pack sent to the CFO and all department heads before this month's finance review meeting. Using the data visible in this workbook, write a commentary covering the following: the Group's overall revenue and expenditure position for the month and year-to-date compared to budget expressed in both RM and percentage terms, a department-by-department narrative for the three departments with the largest adverse variances identifying the specific cost lines driving the overspend and any known one-off factors that explain the variance, a brief positive note on any department that has performed notably well against budget to give a balanced view, and a forward-looking paragraph flagging any upcoming cost pressures or budget risks the leadership team should be aware of in the next one to two months. Write in formal continuous prose without bullet points or numbered lists, use precise RM figures throughout, and target a total length of 300 to 350 words. The tone should be direct and analytical. Department heads will read this before the meeting and it should prompt them to prepare responses on their variances.

Annual Budget Forecast & Scenario PlanningCopilot in Excel

Scenario: The CFO wants three budget scenarios for the next financial year based on different volume and cost assumptions before the Board strategy session.

I am the Group Finance Manager at Contoso Medical Group preparing the annual budget for the next financial year to present at the Board strategy session next month. Using the current year actuals data in this workbook, I need you to build three budget scenarios in a new sheet called Budget Scenarios. Scenario one is the Base Case, which assumes patient volume grows by 5%, medical consumable costs increase by 8% due to continued supply chain pressure, and headcount remains flat. Scenario two is the Optimistic Case, which assumes patient volume grows by 12% following the opening of the new specialist centre, medical consumable costs are contained to 4% growth through renegotiated supplier contracts, and one additional clinical team is hired at a total annual cost of RM 1.2 million. Scenario three is the Stress Case, which assumes patient volume declines by 3% due to increased competition from a new private hospital opening nearby, medical consumable costs increase by 15% due to a supply disruption, and the group incurs a one-off TechMed contract renegotiation cost of RM 200,000. For each scenario present the projected total revenue, total expenditure, EBITDA, and EBITDA margin in a summary table, and add a sensitivity column showing the RM impact versus the Base Case for each scenario. Flag which cost lines are most sensitive to the assumptions so the Board understands where the forecast risk is concentrated.

Cash Flow Forecasting & Working Capital ReviewCopilot in Excel / Analyst Agent

Scenario: Treasury team needs a 13-week cash flow forecast and working capital analysis to present to the CFO ahead of a potential capex commitment.

I am the Group Finance Manager at Contoso Medical Group and we are evaluating a significant capital expenditure commitment that requires me to confirm our cash position and working capital headroom before the CFO can approve it. Using the cash flow data and accounts receivable ageing in this workbook, I need you to produce a 13-week cash flow forecast starting from the current week, projecting weekly opening cash balance, expected collections from insurer and patient receivables based on historical collection rates and the current AR ageing profile, projected operating payments including payroll, supplier payments, and utility costs based on the historical payment pattern, and the closing cash balance for each week. Highlight any week where the projected closing cash balance falls below RM 5 million, which is our minimum liquidity threshold, as these weeks represent a cash risk that needs management action. Then separately calculate our current working capital position showing debtor days, creditor days, and the cash conversion cycle, and benchmark these against the typical range for private hospital groups in Malaysia of 45 to 60 debtor days and 30 to 45 creditor days, flagging any metric that is outside this range and explaining the likely implication for our working capital management.

The GCSE framework + Finance-specific prompt library

The GCSE Prompting Framework

Goal

What do you want Copilot to produce? Be specific about the output type: table, email, summary, analysis.

Context

Who are you? What is the situation? The more context you give, the more relevant the output.

Source

Reference the specific file, data, or knowledge Copilot should use. Never assume it will find it automatically.

Expectation

What format, tone, length, or structure do you expect? E.g. "as a table", "under 150 words", "board-report style".

Example (Bad vs Good):
❌ "Summarise the contract."
✅ "Using the attached TechMed contract Source, identify all clauses that breach Contoso Medical's standard contract policy Context, and present them as a risk table with clause number, risk level, and recommended action Expectation. I am the Finance Manager preparing a report for the CFO Goal Context."
