Exercise 1 · Researcher

Tool: Researcher Agent (Copilot Chat)  ·  25 minutes

Objective

Use the Researcher agent in Copilot Chat to benchmark Contoso Medical's procurement thresholds against regional healthcare industry norms. Researcher goes deeper than standard Copilot Chat — it reasons across multiple sources, critiques its own output, and produces more reliable, cited answers.

Step 1 · Open the Researcher Agent

1
Go to Copilot Chat and click Researcher in the agents panel on the right.
2
Choose your reasoning mode:
🔍 Critique

One model drafts the answer, a second model critiques and improves it. Best for getting a well-reviewed, balanced output on a single question.

⚖️ Model Council

Multiple models answer independently, then a judge model synthesises the best response. Best for complex research where you want diverse perspectives.

Recommended for Task 1: Try Critique first, then re-run with Model Council and compare the outputs.

Task 1 · Industry Benchmarking

With the Researcher agent open, use the prompt below:

I am the Head of Finance at Contoso Medical Group, a private hospital group in Malaysia with four facilities and approximately RM 280 million in annual operating expenditure. Our CFO has asked me to benchmark our procurement governance framework before Thursday's Finance Committee review. I need you to research how comparable private hospital groups in Southeast Asia — specifically IHH Healthcare, KPJ Healthcare, Ramsay Sime Darby, and Bangkok Dusit Medical Services — structure their procurement thresholds, including single-quote purchase limits, the value at which a formal RFQ requiring at least three supplier quotes is triggered, open tender thresholds, standard notice periods required to prevent auto-renewal of vendor contracts, and typical liability cap durations in technology and managed-services agreements. Present your findings as a comparison table with each hospital group as a column and each policy dimension as a row. In a separate paragraph below the table, highlight the two or three areas where our practice is likely to differ most significantly from regional peers, and flag any areas where tighter controls are generally considered better governance practice in the healthcare sector.

What to look for

  • How does our RM 10K single-quote limit compare to peers?
  • Is our 90-day auto-renewal notice standard or above average?
  • Are our liability cap standards (12 months minimum) typical?

Task 2 · Policy Manual Summary

Attach 05_ContosoMedical_FinanceProcurementPolicies.docx to the Researcher agent and use the prompt below.

I am reviewing the attached Contoso Medical Finance & Procurement Policy Manual ahead of a Finance Committee meeting where the CFO will be asking questions about our governance thresholds. I need you to read the full document and extract a concise but complete reference summary covering the CAPEX approval authority matrix showing who must approve at each value band, the exact budget variance percentage thresholds that require escalation and the specific approver or body they must escalate to, the procurement method thresholds that determine when a single quote is sufficient versus when a formal RFQ or open tender is required along with the minimum number of suppliers at each level, and the three mandatory contract standards we must enforce on all vendor agreements covering the minimum liability cap duration, the required auto-renewal notice period, and the maximum permitted annual price escalation. For each item quote the exact policy section number. Format the output as a set of reference tables I can attach to my CFO briefing. If any of these areas are ambiguous or not explicitly addressed in the document, flag them clearly so I can raise them as policy gaps at the committee meeting.

Task 3 · Gap Analysis Setup

Using the Contoso Medical Finance & Procurement Policy Manual I have attached, I need you to build a practical pre-sign-off contract review checklist for Finance Managers on my team who are not legal specialists and will be reviewing vendor contracts on a regular basis. The checklist should have eight specific yes or no questions covering the highest-risk contract areas we deal with most frequently — particularly liability cap adequacy, auto-renewal notice requirements, data and IP ownership, annual price escalation limits, and termination for convenience rights. For each question in the checklist, include the specific policy section number the question is derived from, clear instructions on what language to look for in the contract when checking this item, a plain-English explanation of what would constitute a policy breach, and the exact escalation action the Finance Manager should take if the answer is No. Format it as a table so it can be printed and used as a physical sign-off checklist. The tone should be practical and jargon-free — these are Finance professionals, not lawyers.

Save this checklist — you will use it in Exercise 3.

Exercise 2 · Financial Analysis

Tool: Copilot in Excel  ·  30 minutes

Objective

Open the Budget Variance workbook in Excel. Use Copilot in Excel to surface the worst-performing departments, understand the root causes, score the supplier quotations, and generate ready-to-use CFO commentary for Thursday's committee pack.

Task 1: Budget Variance Analysis

Open 01_ContosoMedical_BudgetVariance.xlsx in Excel. Navigate to the Monthly_PnL sheet and open Copilot (Home ribbon → Copilot).

I am the Head of Finance at Contoso Medical Group and I am preparing the Q1 Finance Committee pack using the budget variance data in this workbook. I need you to analyse the Monthly_PnL sheet and produce a Finance Committee-ready variance summary that shows the Group's overall cost position year-to-date expressed in both RM and as a percentage against budget, identifies the three departments with the largest adverse variances including the variance amount, the variance percentage, and a clear flag where the variance has exceeded the 10% threshold that triggers a mandatory CFO review and corrective action plan under our internal policy, highlights any department where performance is notably favourable versus budget as this is also useful context for the committee, and for the single worst-performing department identifies the largest individual cost line driving the overspend. Present the department analysis as a table with a RAG status column using Green for within 5%, Amber for 5 to 10%, and Red for over 10%. Below each Red department row add a one-sentence plain-English explanation of the likely driver based on the data visible in the sheet.

Task 2: Supplier Quotation Scoring

Switch to 02_ContosoMedical_SupplierQuotations.xlsx, Scoring_Guide sheet.

I am evaluating supplier quotations for surgical consumables at Contoso Medical Group and I need to use this scoring matrix to produce a recommendation I can defend to the CFO and Finance Committee. Using the Scoring_Guide sheet in this workbook, first check every supplier against the mandatory disqualification criteria and clearly identify any supplier that must be eliminated along with the exact criterion they failed. Then for the remaining qualifying suppliers calculate the total weighted score for each one using the criteria weights defined in the sheet and rank them from highest to lowest, showing the individual score for every criterion so the reasoning is fully transparent. Identify the top-ranked supplier and explain the three specific evaluation areas where they most clearly outperform the second-ranked option. Also flag any areas where two suppliers score within five points of each other as these may warrant a clarification round before final award. Present the complete ranked scoring table followed by a short paragraph summarising the recommendation.

Task 3 · CFO Commentary Draft

Stay in 01_ContosoMedical_BudgetVariance.xlsx with Copilot in Excel open.

Using the budget variance data visible in the Monthly_PnL sheet of this workbook, draft the CFO commentary section for Contoso Medical Group's Q1 Finance Committee board pack. The commentary should open by stating the Group's overall cost position for the quarter in both RM and percentage terms against budget, then move into a substantive discussion of the two departments with the most significant adverse variances — naming each department, quantifying the variance, and where the data allows it, identifying the most likely cost driver. It should then briefly acknowledge the one department that has performed notably well against budget to give the committee a balanced view. The commentary must explicitly state which departments have breached the 10% variance threshold that triggers a mandatory corrective action plan under our internal policy, and close with a clear statement of the management actions proposed and the expected timeline for resolution. Write in continuous prose — no bullet points or numbered lists. The tone should be formal and direct, suitable for a Board Audit Committee pack. Target length is 200 to 220 words.

Task 4 · Analyst Agent

The Analyst Agent is a built-in agent in Copilot Chat that can analyse data, build pivot tables, create charts, and run calculations — no Excel skills needed. Open Copilot Chat, select Analyst from the agents panel, and upload 01_ContosoMedical_BudgetVariance.xlsx.

I am uploading the Contoso Medical Group budget variance workbook and I need the Analyst Agent to run a full quantitative analysis that I can present at the Finance Committee on Thursday. Using the Monthly_PnL sheet, build a pivot table showing actual expenditure versus budget for every department across all 12 months with a variance column and a variance percentage column, and apply RAG conditional formatting so cells exceeding 10% adverse variance appear red, 5 to 10% appear amber, and within budget appear green. Then generate a horizontal bar chart ranking the top 5 departments by total year-to-date adverse variance so the committee can see the biggest problems at a glance. Analyse the monthly trend data to identify any department that has overspent against budget for three or more consecutive months, because a persistent pattern like that suggests a structural budget problem rather than a timing issue, and flag those departments separately. Finally, project forward the total financial exposure for the rest of the year if every department currently in breach of the 10% threshold continues overspending at the same monthly run rate — I need this number to brief the CFO on worst-case exposure before the meeting.

Exercise 3 · Contract & Reporting

Tools: Copilot in Word · Copilot Chat · Copilot in Outlook  ·  35 minutes

Objective

Review the TechMed contract for compliance with Contoso Medical's procurement policies. Surface hidden risk clauses, compare them against policy standards, then draft a Risk Summary and a email to the CFO to the CFO.

Task 1: Contract Risk Extraction

Open 03_ContosoMedical_VendorContract.docx in Word. Open Copilot from the Home ribbon.

I need to review this TechMed Healthcare Solutions contract before raising it at Thursday's Finance Committee because our Audit Committee has flagged it as potentially having unfavourable terms. Read the full contract and identify every clause that creates a financial, legal, or operational risk for Contoso Medical. For each risk clause you find, quote the exact problematic contract language, explain in plain English what the risk is and why it matters to a hospital group, estimate the financial or operational exposure it creates for Contoso Medical quantified in RM wherever the contract value makes this calculable, describe what the market-standard or best-practice position for this clause type should be, and suggest alternative wording that would adequately protect Contoso Medical's interests. Assign each risk a rating of HIGH, MEDIUM, or LOW based on the magnitude of potential exposure and likelihood of it being triggered. Present as a table sorted by risk level with the highest risks first so the Finance Committee sees the most critical issues immediately.

Task 2: Risk Summary Document

Using the risk analysis you have just completed on the TechMed contract, now produce a formal Contract Risk Assessment document that I can attach to the Finance Committee board pack. The document should open with an executive summary block showing the vendor name, total contract value, commencement and expiry dates, and critically the auto-renewal trigger date so the committee understands the time pressure. Follow this with a risk register table covering every HIGH and MEDIUM risk clause, showing the clause number, the risk in plain English, the financial exposure in RM, and the specific remediation action I am recommending. Below the risk register include a single-line total showing the combined worst-case financial exposure from all HIGH-risk clauses combined — this is the number the CFO and committee will focus on. Then write a short standalone paragraph giving an overall contract risk rating of High, Medium, or Low with a clear justification. Close with a next steps section naming three specific actions with recommended owners and timelines. Write all narrative sections in formal business English with no bullet points as this document will be read by Board members.

Tip: Copy Copilot's output and paste into a new Word doc. Then use Copilot to refine the tone: "Rewrite this in a more formal board-report style."

Task 3: CFO Email Draft

Open Outlook and start a new email to your CFO (use your facilitator's email for demo purposes). Open Copilot Draft with me.

Draft an email from me, Nurul, Head of Finance at Contoso Medical Group, to the Group CFO flagging an urgent issue with the TechMed Healthcare Solutions contract that needs to be addressed before Thursday's Finance Committee and before the contract auto-renews. The email should open with a single sentence that tells the CFO exactly what the problem is and why it cannot wait, open the body with a brief factual statement of the contract value and the auto-renewal date so the time pressure is immediately clear, then walk through the three HIGH-risk clauses in a concise and readable way — each one should mention the clause number, what the issue is in plain English, and the financial exposure or policy gap it creates for Contoso Medical. After covering the HIGH risks, briefly note the MEDIUM-risk clause so the CFO has the full picture. State the combined worst-case financial exposure from all HIGH risks in a single RM figure. Make a clear and specific recommendation on what action to take — do not leave it open-ended. Close with a direct ask of the CFO stating exactly what decision or instruction I need from them and by when. Keep the total email under 220 words and write it in a professional but direct senior-to-senior tone.

Prompting Tips

The GCSE framework + Finance-specific prompt library

The GCSE Prompting Framework

G, Goal

What do you want Copilot to produce? Be specific about the output type: table, email, summary, analysis.

C, Context

Who are you? What is the situation? The more context you give, the more relevant the output.

S, Source

Reference the specific file, data, or knowledge Copilot should use. Never assume it will find it automatically.

E, Expectation

What format, tone, length, or structure do you expect? E.g. "as a table", "under 150 words", "board-report style".

Example (Bad vs Good):
❌ "Summarise the contract."
✅ "Using the attached TechMed contract (Source), identify all clauses that breach Contoso Medical's standard contract policy (Context), and present them as a risk table with clause number, risk level, and recommended action (Expectation). I am the Finance Manager preparing a report for the CFO (Goal + Context)."

Finance Prompt Library

Budget Variance Commentary
Write a CFO commentary for [department] showing an adverse variance of [X]% vs budget in [month]. Explain likely causes and management actions. Keep it under 100 words.
Contract Risk Scan
Review this contract and flag any clauses relating to: liability cap, auto-renewal, price escalation, data ownership, and IP. Rate each as HIGH/MEDIUM/LOW risk.
Supplier Comparison
Compare these [N] supplier quotes based on: price, delivery lead time, quality certification, and payment terms. Recommend the best value option with justification.
Board Summary Draft
Draft a one-page board summary covering [topic]. Include: executive summary (3 sentences), key findings (bullet list), financial impact, and recommended board resolution.
Policy Q&A
Based on the [Policy Manual], what is the correct approval process for [scenario]? Quote the relevant policy section and state exactly who must approve.
Action Item Extraction
Extract all action items from this meeting transcript. For each, provide: action description, owner, due date, and priority (High/Medium/Low). Present as a table.
